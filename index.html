How do I get this file?
  1. Instagram app → Settings and activity → Accounts Center
  2. Your information and permissions → Download your information
  3. Format: JSON (not HTML), Date range: All time, include Followers and following
  4. When the email arrives, download the .zip and add it here.

You can add the whole .zip, or just following.json and followers_1.json (add all followers_*.json if there are several).

3. Unfollow safely

Do about 100–120 per day, no more. If Instagram ever says "Try again later," stop for 24–48 hours.
Never use an app or extension that asks for your Instagram password. This tool never needs it, and neither should anything else.
